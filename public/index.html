HOW IT WORKS

Your rating.
Whoever your partner.

Just like chess: beat someone higher-rated to gain more Elo. They lose the same amount. A favorite winning earns less. BFL score margin also counts.

  1. Choose four friends for a game.
  2. Record the final BFL score.
  3. Save the final football score.
  4. Each player’s Elo updates.

Both partners receive the same Elo change, based on the two sides’ average ratings. Upsets earn more. Larger score margins count, up to a 28-point cap.

BRING YOUR HISTORY

Start with the old games.

Import a CSV of past results or restore players and matches from a league JSON backup. Your existing games stay saved.

↓ Download blank CSV template

CSV columns: id, played_at, team_a, team_b, winner, score_a, score_b, event. Use | between teammates, and A, B, or DRAW for the result. Scores are final BFL point totals. Older win/loss-only rows may leave both scores blank. Dates need a timezone, e.g. 2026-11-01T20:00:00Z.

Repeated imports skip identical match IDs. Conflicting results are rejected; correct the saved game in Match history. Without an ID, date + teams + event identify a game. Use distinct IDs for repeated games at the same time.
